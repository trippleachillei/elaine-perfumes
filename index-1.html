The house

More than a fragrance.

A scent is one of the only things that can put you back in a room you left years ago. We think about that every time we sit down to build a formula — not what it smells like on a card, but what it will mean to someone in five years.

Every ÉLANE fragrance is composed by hand in small batches, tested on skin rather than paper, and given time to settle before it's approved. We'd rather release one composition a year that means something than six that don't.

This isn't about being noticed. It's about being remembered.

— The ÉLANE atelier
